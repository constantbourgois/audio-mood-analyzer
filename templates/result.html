The boundaries and the groups are measured: sections are cut where the texture of the recording changes, and sections that resemble each other share a group letter — so "group B appears three times" is data. The names are a guess on top of that: nothing in the signal says "chorus", so a quiet opening is called an intro, the loudest repeated group the chorus, and so on. On an unusual arrangement the names can be wrong even when the boundaries are right — that is what the group letter is there for.
