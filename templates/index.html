Audio Analyser

Which instruments play, and when, in a recording (guitar, vocals, ...).

Instruments are recognised by YAMNet (a general-purpose audio tagger), not by ear: a quiet instrument under a loud one is often missed, and a distorted guitar can read as a synthesizer. Only instrument recognition runs here for now — key, tempo, mode, chords and song structure are disabled on this deployment to fit a memory-limited free hosting plan.
