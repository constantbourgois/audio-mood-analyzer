How it works

Layers are measured from the signal — frequency register plus whether the sound sustains or arrives as sharp attacks — so bass, drums and sustained harmony are separated without guessing instrument names. Tempo comes from how regularly the sound gets sudden attacks; the key from the track's overall pitch content. Chords and song structure are disabled on this deployment to fit a memory-limited free hosting plan.
